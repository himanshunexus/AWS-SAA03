## Answer limitations

Source notes are retained, not silently presented as verified answers. Suggested answers are explicitly labeled. Q198 requires compatibility checks; Q235 needs bidirectional-write planning; Q257 mixes metrics with events; Q366 has an incomplete authorization design; Q390 requires explicit durability decisions; Q543 has ambiguous organization-account choices. Those caveats remain with their questions.

Historical Snow devices, product names, fixed quotas, pricing and security-certification claims require current documentation checks. The revision notes avoid relying on historical capacities or fixed prices as universal rules. No claim is made that every source answer or linked guide has been independently verified.
